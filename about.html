Our Story

Fewer, Better Things

Why we started Forme

Forme began with a frustration many people share: a closet full of clothes that didn't last, made from fabrics we couldn't name, sold to us on a marketing cycle designed to feel outdated in six weeks.

We wanted to build something slower. Fewer releases, better materials, and pieces designed to be worn for years rather than a season. Every product goes through several rounds of fit testing before it reaches the shop, and every fabric is chosen for how it wears over time, not just how it photographs.

We're still a small team, which means every decision, from the stitching on a hem to the box your order arrives in, gets made by someone who actually cares about it.

What we believe

The principles behind every piece

Made to last

We choose fabric weight and construction for durability first, trend second.

Slower by design

A handful of considered collections a year rather than a constant churn of newness.

Honest pricing

No inflated list prices designed to make a sale feel bigger than it is.
